About Us

Chadha Developers and Infrastructure Limited is one of the pioneering Indian real estate developers, established in 2006. With a strong presence across Mumbai, Punjab, and Delhi, the company has built a reputation for delivering affordable, smart, and modern living spaces. Focused on creating iconic residential and commercial developments, the company is now expanding its vision towards rejuvenating lifestyle experiences in Mumbai suburbs.
